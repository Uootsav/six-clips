FAQ

Questions, answered.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Yes. Windows shows this for every new app that doesn't have a paid code-signing certificate yet. Click More info → Run anyway. The source code is public on GitHub for anyone to check.

How do I install it?

Download the ZIP, right-click it and choose Extract All, then open SIX Clips.exe inside the folder. Don't run it from inside the ZIP.

Do I need internet?

Only to download videos from links, and once on the first run to download the speech model (about 0.5 GB for English or 1.6 GB for other languages). After that, video files work fully offline.

How long does it take?

Transcription is the slowest step. On a laptop without an NVIDIA graphics card it can take about as long as the video. Progress bars show every step, and re-running the same video skips finished steps.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with 8 GB of RAM or more. An NVIDIA graphics card makes it much faster but isn't required.

Is there a Mac or phone version?

Not yet. SIX Clips is Windows-only for now.

Can I clip any video?

Only clip videos you own or have permission to use, such as your own channel, a creator's clipping campaign or licensed content. You're responsible for following copyright law and each platform's terms.

Does SIX Clips collect my data?

No. It runs on your PC and collects no videos or personal data. The optional AI mode sends only transcript text to the AI provider you choose, using your own key.
