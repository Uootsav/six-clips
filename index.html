FAQ

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Yes. Windows shows this for every new app that doesn't have a paid code-signing certificate yet. Click More info → Run anyway. The full source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check it.

Do I need internet?

Only to download videos from links, and once on the first run to download the speech model (about 0.5 GB for English or 1.6 GB for other languages). After that, video files work fully offline.

How long does it take?

Transcribing is the slowest step. On a laptop without an NVIDIA graphics card it can take about as long as the video. The app shows a progress bar for every step. If you re-run the same video, it skips the steps that are already done.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and 8 GB of RAM or more. An NVIDIA graphics card makes it much faster but isn't required.

Which languages are supported?

99 languages for speech and captions, including English, Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Spanish, Arabic and more. You can also translate captions to English.

Is there a Mac or phone version?

Not yet. SIX Clips is Windows-only for now.

Can I clip any YouTube video?

Only clip videos you own or have permission to use, such as your own channel, a creator's clipping campaign or licensed content. You're responsible for following copyright law and each platform's terms.

Does SIX Clips collect my data?

No. SIX Clips runs on your PC and collects no videos or personal data. The optional AI mode sends only transcript text to the AI provider you choose, using your own key.
