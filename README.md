# SIX Clips 🌴

**Long video in. Six viral clips out.** A free AI clip maker for Windows.

Paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link, or drop a video file. SIX Clips finds the best moments, reframes them to **9:16** and adds **animated word-by-word subtitles**, ready for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

## Features
- Works with links from YouTube, Twitch, Kick, X, Facebook and Instagram, or with local video files
- Finds complete moments: hooks, stories, punchlines and audience laughs, each with a virality score
- Face tracking, facecam + gameplay split, or blurred-background layouts
- Animated captions in 99 languages, including Hindi, Gujarati and Hinglish
- Runs on your PC: private, no upload, no watermark, no subscription
- Optional AI mode with your own free Google Gemini key
- Five themes

## Install
1. Download `SIXClips_Portable.zip.` from [Releases](https://github.com/uootsav/six-clips/releases/latest).
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**. This appears for new apps without a paid signing certificate.
3. The first run downloads the speech model once (0.5–1.6 GB).

Requires Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and 8 GB of RAM or more. An NVIDIA GPU is optional and makes it faster.
